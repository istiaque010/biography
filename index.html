Research areas

Four threads that keep meeting in the middle: trust, identity and verification.

Read in depth →

Security of AI systems

LLM prompt safety and guardrails, agentic AI, self-sovereign identity for AI agents, and explainable AI for security decisions.

LLM and generative model architectures

A newer direction: building LLMs rather than only defending them — autoregressive language model architectures and diffusion-based generative models, and how each behaves once deployed.

Privacy-preserving identity

Self-sovereign identity, decentralized identifiers, verifiable credentials, eKYC, zero-knowledge circuits and secure multi-party computation.

Blockchain and secure communication

Consensus algorithms, smart-contract security, post-quantum-safe design, Web3 and decentralized application infrastructure.
